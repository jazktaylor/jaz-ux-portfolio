UX CASE STUDY

DesignHive

A collaborative UI/UX workspace built around the idea of a creative community.

Overview

DesignHive is a collaborative UI/UX workspace built around the idea of a creative community. Instead of only focusing on editing designs together, DesignHive helps teams share ideas, learn from each other, build reputations, and move projects through structured stages.

Purpose

The purpose of DesignHive is to make UI design collaborative from the beginning instead of waiting until a design is nearly finished to receive feedback.

Problem Statement

UI/UX designers often receive meaningful feedback only after a design is already significantly developed. When collaboration happens primarily around completed or nearly completed designs, opportunities to share ideas, learn from other designers, and improve concepts earlier in the process can be missed.

Designers need a collaborative environment that encourages feedback and knowledge sharing throughout the design process—not only at the final stages.

Collaboration

Encourage designers to share ideas and work together throughout the design process.

Feedback

Make feedback part of the creative process instead of something saved for the end.

Creative Community

Create opportunities for designers to learn, contribute, and build their reputation.
